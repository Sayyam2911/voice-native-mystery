# Case source research

Status: the source approach and standalone present-day presentation are accepted. “The Adventure of the Abbey Grange” is selected as the first source. Its [present-day case bible](cases/grange-house.md) is a review draft, not yet an approved playable case. Other stories remain candidates. Adapted cases will credit their literary sources.

## Selection criteria

- Fictional murder with a known, internally consistent solution.
- Enough people and conflicting accounts for voice interviews.
- Evidence the player can inspect and connect to claims.
- A compact plot that can support a 15–30 minute investigation after adaptation.
- A contemporary setting can preserve the causal logic without introducing obvious modern-technology contradictions.
- Publication/adaptation rights can be checked for intended release locations.

## Recommended sources

[The Return of Sherlock Holmes](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/108) by Arthur Conan Doyle is a collection of 13 short detective stories. The project owner accepts contemporary adaptations of older solved detective stories. The collection contains several promising murder cases, though not all 13 stories suit the game.

| Story | Why it fits | Work required |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [The Adventure of the Abbey Grange](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/108/pg108-images.html) | A death, staged burglary, two coordinated false accounts, physical evidence that undermines them, and a final confrontation. Selected first source for testimony and contradiction gameplay. | Put the person who struck the fatal blow in the accessible cast earlier; expand the investigative paths; replace or justify period details. The accepted objective is to establish who caused the death and how the scene was staged, without a legal verdict. |
| [The Adventure of Black Peter](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/108/pg108-images.html) | A murder, a compelling false suspect, several physical clues, and a later alternative explanation. Possible second case. | The actual killer appears late in the original; the roster and evidence discovery need restructuring. Maritime details need a contemporary setting that preserves their logic. |
| [The Boscombe Valley Mystery](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/1661/pg1661-images.html), from [The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/1661) | A murder with an apparently compelling accusation, multiple early witness accounts, a concealed motive, and physical evidence that tests the testimony. Strong third candidate for voice interrogation. | Rework period landholding, communication, and clue details, especially deductions that depend on dated expressions or assumptions. Keep the actual culprit available to question. |
| [The Adventure of the Golden Pince-Nez](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/108/pg108-images.html) | A murder in a small household and a concealed relationship. Reserve option. | Some deductions rely on dated assumptions; the culprit's availability and proof path need a substantial rewrite. |

[A Study in Scarlet](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/244), currently open in the browser, is a full novel with an extended backstory. It is a weaker first case for a short voice investigation.

## Rights note

Project Gutenberg identifies this Doyle collection as [public domain in the USA](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/108). Its notice asks users outside the USA to check local law. [India's Copyright Act, section 22](https://copyright.gov.in/Copyright_Act_1957/chapter_v.html), gives the general term for literary works published during an author's life as 60 years starting in the year after death; Gutenberg lists Doyle's death in 1930. Distribution locations and any borrowed modern adaptations still need checking before publication.

## Proposed initial catalog

“The Adventure of the Abbey Grange” is the first selected source and is being adapted to prove the voice interrogation format. “The Boscombe Valley Mystery” and “Black Peter” are candidates for cases two and three. “The Golden Pince-Nez” is a reserve fourth option, with higher adaptation effort. The rest of the lineup remains open for discussion.
