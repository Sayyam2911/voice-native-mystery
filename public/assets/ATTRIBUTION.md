# Character portraits

The five portrait images in `avatars/` were created with the built-in image-generation tool for this project. They depict fictional characters, not photographs of identifiable real people. They are locally bundled rather than hotlinked.

`portrait-manifest.json` records the saved filenames, generation mode, and complete prompts. The original generated PNGs remain in the owner's Codex generated-images directory.

The initially downloaded DiceBear illustrations were replaced at the owner's request and are not used or shipped by the application.
