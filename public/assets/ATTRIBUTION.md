# Character portraits

The thirteen portrait images in `avatars/` were created with the built-in image-generation tool for this project. They depict fictional characters, not photographs of identifiable real people. They are locally bundled rather than hotlinked. Detective Rowan's portrait is reused across all three cases.

`portrait-manifest.json` and `additional-portrait-manifest.json` record the saved filenames, generation mode, and complete prompts. The original generated PNGs remain in the owner's Codex generated-images directory.

The initially downloaded DiceBear illustrations were replaced at the owner's request and are not used or shipped by the application.
